Who we are

An independent sourcing partner—not a manufacturer.

COCOVANTA connects overseas buyers with selected Indonesian product sources. Our role may include clarifying requirements, identifying potential suppliers, coordinating product information and supporting commercial communication. The contractual seller, manufacturer, exporter of record and payment beneficiary are identified for each transaction.
