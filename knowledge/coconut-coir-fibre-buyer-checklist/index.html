Coconut Coir Fibre: Buyer Checklist

Key questions for buyers defining coconut coir fibre form, cleanliness, fibre characteristics and intended application.

COCOVANTA Knowledge Centre · General B2B information

Start with application

Coir fibre may be used in different natural-fibre applications. Explain the finished product or process so that the source can assess the relevant form.

Describe the material

Potential criteria include fibre length, thickness, moisture, colour, cleanliness, dust content and bale or bundle presentation. Do not assume a standard grade without agreement.

Confirm sample and inspection

Agree how a sample will be selected, who evaluates it and whether an inspection is required before shipment.

Document acceptance

Record the agreed product description, criteria, packaging and responsibilities in the purchase documentation.

This article is general information, not a technical certificate, legal opinion, customs ruling or confirmation of product availability. Requirements must be verified for the actual product and destination.
