VCO, RBD and Crude Coconut Oil: Understanding the Categories

A practical overview of three coconut oil product categories and the importance of defining the product before sourcing.

COCOVANTA Knowledge Centre · General B2B information

Virgin Coconut Oil

Virgin coconut oil is a distinct coconut oil category. The buyer should state the required processing identity, intended use and relevant quality criteria.

RBD Coconut Oil

RBD refers to refined, bleached and deodorized coconut oil. Buyers should confirm the precise grade and specification required for their application.

Crude Coconut Oil

Crude coconut oil is an unrefined category. Extraction and handling details can vary, so the product definition should be agreed before quotation.

Do not assume equivalence

These categories are not interchangeable. Product descriptions, tests, labels and commercial documents should identify the specific category being offered.

This article is general information, not a technical certificate, legal opinion, customs ruling or confirmation of product availability. Requirements must be verified for the actual product and destination.
