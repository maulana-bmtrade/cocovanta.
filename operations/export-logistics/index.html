EXPORT COORDINATION

Export Logistics & Port Handling

Understand the logistics and port-handling stages that may be coordinated by the parties named in a transaction.

Responsibilities are named before shipment

COCOVANTA may facilitate communication around shipment planning, but does not automatically act as exporter of record, customs declarant, freight forwarder, carrier, insurer or document issuer. The responsible parties, Incoterm and named place, booking details, charges and documents must be stated clearly in the transaction.

Inland transport and port

Confirm pickup point, transport provider, port of loading, cut-off dates and handling responsibilities.

Export documentation

The authorized party prepares and issues commercial, customs, origin, inspection and transport documents as applicable.

Freight and delivery terms

Agree the Incoterm version, named place or port, freight basis, insurance responsibility, transit assumptions and destination handling.

COCOVANTA acts as an independent trading and sourcing business. Any proposed supplier, facility, service provider, product specification and transaction responsibility must be verified and agreed individually before a commercial commitment.
