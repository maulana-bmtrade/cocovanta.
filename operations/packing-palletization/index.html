PACKING PREPARATION

Packaging & Palletization

Explore packaging, labelling, palletization and cargo preparation topics to align before an order is confirmed.

Packaging is agreed against the buyer’s brief

Packaging is not a fixed COCOVANTA-owned specification. The final bag, carton, drum, inner liner, pallet, label, marks and protective materials must be confirmed with the actual supplier and buyer. Any brand application, including COCOVANTA marks, must be authorized by the relevant rights holder and agreed by the responsible parties.

Packaging format

Choose the appropriate bag, carton, drum or bulk format based on product, handling, destination and buyer requirements.

Pallet and protection

Confirm pallet dimensions, wrapping, moisture protection, load stability and any destination-specific requirements.

Labels and shipping marks

Approve product details, net and gross weight, lot or batch references, handling marks and branding before production or packing.

COCOVANTA acts as an independent trading and sourcing business. Any proposed supplier, facility, service provider, product specification and transaction responsibility must be verified and agreed individually before a commercial commitment.
