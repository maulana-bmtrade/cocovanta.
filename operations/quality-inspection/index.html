QUALITY COORDINATION

Quality Inspection & Verification

How buyer specifications, samples, inspection scope and lot-specific evidence can be discussed before shipment.

Quality claims require relevant evidence

COCOVANTA does not operate its own laboratory and does not issue independent quality certificates. It can help coordinate buyer requirements with a proposed supplier and, where agreed, an independent inspection or testing provider. A result should be linked to the relevant product lot, test method, date and issuing party.

Specification agreement

Define measurable parameters, tolerances, test methods, sampling procedure and acceptance criteria before order confirmation.

Sample and inspection

Agree who selects and seals samples, who performs testing, where inspection occurs and who bears the cost.

Report and claim process

Check the report issuer, date, method, sample identity and lot reference. Contract terms should state the claim window and remedy.

COCOVANTA acts as an independent trading and sourcing business. Any proposed supplier, facility, service provider, product specification and transaction responsibility must be verified and agreed individually before a commercial commitment.
