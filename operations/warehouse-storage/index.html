STORAGE & HANDLING

Warehouse & Storage

A dedicated overview of warehouse storage considerations for coconut-derived products before dispatch.

Storage arrangements depend on the supplier and product

Warehouse locations, operators and storage conditions are not owned or controlled by COCOVANTA. Storage arrangements are confirmed with the named supplier or logistics party for the specific order. Photographs on this page provide general visual context and are not proof of a particular supplier’s warehouse.

Facility and location

The warehouse operator, address, access arrangements and relationship to the offered goods should be confirmed.

Storage conditions

Moisture protection, ventilation, cleanliness, stacking, segregation and product-specific handling should be reviewed.

Stock and traceability

Quantity, lot identification, storage period and readiness for dispatch should be supported by current information.

COCOVANTA acts as an independent trading and sourcing business. Any proposed supplier, facility, service provider, product specification and transaction responsibility must be verified and agreed individually before a commercial commitment.
