Cocopeat

Cocopeat is a coir-derived growing-medium material. Buyers should define the intended use and required product form before sourcing.

Product characteristics

Potential parameters include particle size, moisture, electrical conductivity, pH, expansion, compression, fibre content, contamination and packaging. Measurement methods and acceptance ranges are agreed with the buyer.

Potential applications

Horticulture, nurseries, substrate blending and growing-media applications depend on grade and performance requirements.

Available product forms and packaging

Forms, dimensions, packing configuration, labelling, palletization and minimum order quantity are not published as fixed offers. They are confirmed with the supplier for the requested product and destination.
