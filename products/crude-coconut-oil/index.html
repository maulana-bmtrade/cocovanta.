Product introduction

Crude Coconut Oil

Crude coconut oil refers to oil before refining, but exact extraction, handling and quality profile depend on source and process. Buyer and supplier must align the product definition.

Product characteristics

Potential review items include free fatty acid, moisture, impurities, colour, odour, fatty-acid profile, contamination, storage conditions and transport method. No technical values are published without lot evidence.

Potential applications

Potential refining or industrial uses depend on buyer process and product suitability.

Crude Coconut Oil: Standard and Premium Specification Targets

Crude coconut oil buyers should define the FFA basis, moisture and impurities, sediment, fatty-acid profile and intended downstream use before comparing offers. A quoted FFA value is not meaningful without its expression basis and test method. COCOVANTA coordinates the specification exchange with potential suppliers and encourages lot-specific analysis before contractual acceptance.

Standard sourcing target

A practical starting point for buyer discussions. Final limits must match the application, supplier evidence and destination requirements.

Premium sourcing target

A tighter target for buyers seeking more demanding acceptance criteria. Premium is a negotiated grade description, not a universal certification or guarantee.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Buying Crude Coconut Oil from Indonesia: What Importers Should Check

When comparing crude coconut oil from Indonesia offers, buyers should compare like-for-like specifications, request the same test basis and confirm packing, shipment and documentation requirements. COCOVANTA helps structure these questions before a supplier quotation is treated as commercially actionable.

Is crude coconut oil automatically suitable for food processing?

No. Suitability depends on processing, contamination controls, test results and applicable food regulations in the destination market.

What does FFA mean in a crude coconut oil offer?

FFA means free fatty acid. The expression basis and test method should be stated, since results can be reported on different bases.

Available product forms and packaging

Forms, dimensions, packing configuration, labelling, palletization and minimum order quantity are not published as fixed offers. They are confirmed with the supplier for the requested product and destination.

Technical specification

There is no universal specification suitable for every buyer or application. Please provide your target parameters, testing methods, intended use and destination requirements. COCOVANTA will review whether an external supplier can respond with relevant evidence.

Quality coordination approach

  1. Document the buyer's acceptance criteria.
  2. Request current supplier and lot information.
  3. Agree sample or inspection options where possible.
  4. Identify the responsible testing or inspection party.
  5. Record the accepted specification and responsibilities in the commercial documents.
