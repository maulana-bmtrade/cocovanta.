COCOVANTA · Indonesia

Export and packaging are coordinated by agreement

Packaging and shipment arrangements depend on the actual product, supplier, destination rules, carrier requirements and agreed Incoterm.

Packaging brief

Discuss unit size, inner and outer packaging, palletization, labelling, moisture protection, handling and destination requirements. Final details require supplier confirmation.

Export responsibilities

The exporter of record, customs declarant, document issuer, freight arranger, insurance party and payment beneficiary must be named for each transaction. COCOVANTA does not claim to perform regulated tasks without authorization.

Shipment preparation

Packing lists, invoices, origin documents, permits, inspection and transport documents are handled by the parties authorized and named in the transaction.

COCOVANTA is an independent trading and sourcing business, not a manufacturer. Availability, specifications and transaction roles are confirmed individually.
