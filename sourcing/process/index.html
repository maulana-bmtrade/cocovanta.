A structured sourcing process

The process begins with the buyer’s product brief and proceeds only as supplier feasibility and transaction responsibilities become clear.

01 · Buyer brief

Clarify product definition, intended use, quantity and unit, specifications, packaging, destination and timing.

02 · Feasibility

Explore potential external suppliers and request current confirmation of product, lot and availability.

03 · Evidence and quality

Coordinate available product information, sample review, inspection options and supporting documents.

04 · Commercial alignment

Prepare a quotation only when seller identity, product basis, Incoterm and named place, payment, validity and responsibilities can be stated.

05 · Order coordination

Coordinate agreed milestones with the named supplier, exporter, logistics provider and other transaction parties.

COCOVANTA is an independent trading and sourcing business, not a manufacturer. Availability, specifications and transaction roles are confirmed individually.

How coordination works

Four practical stages before a commercial commitment

Each stage is completed only when the relevant information can be verified and the parties agree on the next step.

1. Define the requirement

Clarify product form, specification, quantity, intended use, packaging and destination.

2. Check supplier feasibility

Discuss capability, sample options, test methods and supporting information with the proposed supplier.

3. Align commercial details

Confirm seller identity, price basis, responsibilities, payment terms and shipment assumptions in writing.

4. Coordinate fulfilment

Coordinate agreed preparation, documentation and logistics with the responsible parties.
