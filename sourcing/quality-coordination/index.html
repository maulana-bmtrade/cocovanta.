Quality requirements are agreed before a purchase

COCOVANTA can help translate buyer requirements into a documented checklist and coordinate responses from external suppliers. It does not operate its own laboratory or certify product quality.

What may be coordinated

Product definitions, buyer specifications, lot identification, sample pathways, inspection scope, test method, document issuer and report date—where available and agreed.

Evidence discipline

A test report is meaningful only when its issuer, method, scope, date and relationship to the offered lot are understood. No generic image or historical report substitutes for lot-specific evidence.

Acceptance

Buyer and seller should agree acceptance criteria, sampling, inspection responsibility, claim window and remedy in the contract.

COCOVANTA is an independent trading and sourcing business, not a manufacturer. Availability, specifications and transaction roles are confirmed individually.
